# PRODUCT.md — SmartCare

## Product
SmartCare is an AI-assisted learning and progress companion for children with special needs at Malvar Assisted Facility and Therapy Center.

## Users
- **Head Doctor** (web): admin, checklists, facility reports
- **Psychometrician** (web): assessments, severity, child history
- **Teacher** (mobile): AI review, therapy schedule, materials, medication schedules
- **Caretaker** (mobile): medication administration logs
- **Parent** (mobile): progress, therapy updates, feedback

Operational test matrix: `specs/001-smartcare-platform/spec.md` § Role Operational Sustainability Tests.

## Mode
Primary product surfaces are **Operate** (task completion): scanability, consistency, calm clinical tone. Login/onboarding are supportive, not marketing-persuade.

## Non-goals
No autonomous diagnosis, no medication prescribing by AI, no multi-facility deployment, no wearables.

## Design authority
Follow `design-system/smartcare/MASTER.md` and `DESIGN.md`. Prefer accessible cyan + health green palette; Figtree / Noto Sans.
