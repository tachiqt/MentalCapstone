# DESIGN.md — SmartCare

## Visual world
Calm clinical companion. Accessible & ethical. High contrast, large readable type, clear focus rings, reduced motion respect.

## Tokens (from UI/UX Pro Max MASTER)
- Primary: `#0891B2`
- Secondary: `#22D3EE`
- Accent: `#059669`
- Background: `#ECFEFF`
- Foreground: `#164E63`
- Muted: `#E8F1F6`
- Border: `#A5F3FC`
- Destructive: `#DC2626`
- Fonts: Figtree (UI), Noto Sans (body)

## Anti-patterns
No neon, no motion-heavy chrome, no AI purple/pink gradients, no emoji-as-icons, no nested card spam.

## Surfaces
- Web dashboards: Operate — dense but clear tables/forms for Head Doctor & Psychometrician
- Mobile: Operate — large touch targets (44px+), simple navigation for Teacher/Caretaker/Parent

## Motion
150–300ms transitions only; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
