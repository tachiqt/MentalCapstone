How to use SmartCare

A complete guide for Head Doctors and Psychometricians on the web dashboard. Teachers, caretakers, and parents work in the mobile app.

01

Getting started

Sign in, navigate the workspace, and open Settings.

Use the username and password created by your Head Doctor. After email verification (when required), you land on Care overview.

  • The left sidebar opens each workspace: Overview, Children, Assessments, Checklists, and more based on your role.
  • Collapse the sidebar anytime if you need a wider layout. Compact navigation can also be saved in Settings.
  • Your name in the top-right opens Settings for profile, password, alerts, and (for Head Doctors) facility defaults.
  • On Overview, the Priority queue highlights items that need review, and Weekly brief jumps to exportable reports.
Tip: If login is blocked, verify your email first or ask the Head Doctor to resend the verification message from Staff.
02

Search and notifications

Find records quickly and stay on top of live activity.

The top search bar finds children, therapy sessions, and (for Head Doctors) staff accounts.

  • Type at least two letters, then open a result to jump straight into that record.
  • Click the bell to open notifications. The panel stays closed until you open it.
  • Notifications cover record changes, successful saves, due therapy sessions, and medication times of day.
  • In Settings → Alerts, you can turn record-change and schedule reminders on or off.
Note: Schedule reminders fire when a session or medication window is due, so keep timezone and reminder lead minutes correct in Facility configuration.
03

Children registry

Register profiles and review the full care record.

Children are the center of SmartCare. Every assessment, recommendation, therapy plan, and medication schedule links back to a child profile.

  • Register a child with name, optional diagnosis/notes, age details, and active status.
  • Open a profile to review assessments, AI recommendations, assigned staff, and related history.
  • Keep inactive profiles marked clearly so Overview priority items and reports stay accurate.
  • Psychometricians use the registry to pick the right child before scoring a checklist.
04

Checklists and assessments

Score carefully, generate AI drafts, and require human review.

Clinical workflow is split by role so scoring and planning stay accountable.

  • Head Doctors create and maintain checklists (items, scoring guidance, active status).
  • Psychometricians select a child and checklist, score items (typically 0–3), set severity, and submit.
  • When scores are ready, request an AI recommendation. Treat it as a draft, not a diagnosis.
  • Teachers review recommendations in the mobile app. Only after review should recommendations guide therapy plans.
  • Use Assessments to revisit recent submissions, AI status (needs AI / awaiting review / approved / modified), and child linkage.
Safety: AI output must never replace clinical judgment. A person must review before it becomes an active plan.
05

Therapy and medication

Schedule sessions, set durations, and keep dose windows visible.

Teachers schedule therapy sessions from the mobile app. Head Doctors use the web calendar for oversight and still manage medication schedules.

  • Review upcoming and completed sessions on the Therapy calendar (view only).
  • Send parent progress updates from the Dashboard or a child profile Progress tab.
  • When a session time is reached, SmartCare posts a notification for staff who opted into schedule alerts.
  • Configure medication schedules with times of day. Caretakers log doses (given/skipped) in the mobile app.
  • Dose logs and due reminders surface in your notification list so gaps are easier to catch.
  • Overview session health and upcoming sessions help you see completion vs. scheduled load at a glance.
06

Reports, staff, and materials

Export briefs, manage accounts, and share learning resources.

  • Reports: Export facility or child briefs as PDF/CSV from Reports, or use Weekly brief on Overview.
  • Staff: Create accounts, assign roles, resend verification email, and deactivate users you no longer need.
  • Materials: Upload or manage learning materials that teachers can open on mobile.
  • Settings → Facility: Facility name, timezone, default session length, and reminder lead minutes.
Access: New staff must verify email before first sign-in. Unverified users appear clearly in Staff.
07

Who uses which app

Web for clinical leadership; mobile for day-to-day care.

Head Doctor

Full web workspace: staff, schedules, materials, facility settings, reports, and oversight of assessments.

Psychometrician

Web access for children, checklists, assessments, AI drafts, and child reports.

Teacher

Mobile app: review AI recommendations, therapy sessions, materials, and medication visibility for assigned children.

Caretaker

Mobile app: medication administration logs and child care routines.

Parent

Mobile app: progress, care updates, therapy visibility, and messaging shared with the care team.

08

Tips and safety

Keep the facility data clean, private, and actionable.

  • Sign out other devices from Settings if a shared computer was used.
  • Prefer clear diagnosis/care notes so mobile roles understand context without guessing.
  • Review Priority queue daily — pending AI reviews and inactive profiles accumulate quickly.
  • Never share passwords. Use unique accounts per staff member.
  • Treat exports as confidential: store PDFs/CSVs only on approved facility systems.
Need help? Contact your Head Doctor for account access, or your facility admin if the API/server is unavailable.
